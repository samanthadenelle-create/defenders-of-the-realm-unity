# Defenders of the Realm — Intro Video
## First 10 Seconds: Shot Direction + Voice Over

---

## SHOT 1 — THE WORLD (0:00 – 0:06)

**Duration:** 6 seconds

**Camera:**
Wide establishing shot. The valley of Elarion at dusk. Camera is elevated — looking down into the valley from the ridge above the village. Slow push in toward the Heartwood at centre.

**What's in frame:**
The village walls, small and warm against the approaching dark. The Heartwood at the centre of the village — pale stone cathedral, violet light glowing from within. The sky at the edges of the frame is dark and moving, like something is in it. The valley floor is lit amber-green from the last of the sun. It looks beautiful. It looks like it won't last.

**Sound:**
No music. A single low, sustained musical note — the Heartwood's chord. It fills the frame the way the light does. This is the only sound.

**NARRATOR VOICE OVER:**
*(Warm. Unhurried. Older. Not epic — intimate, like someone telling you something true.)*

> *"The Heart-Tree burned a hundred winters ago.*

*(beat — 1 second of just the held note)*

> *The Folk built a spire over what remained —*
> *and bound the last of its song inside."*

**Direction note:** The narrator does not rush. There is a natural pause after the first sentence — let the image breathe. The word "song" lands on the Heartwood's violet light brightening slightly.

---

## SHOT 2 — THRAIN (0:06 – 0:10)

**Duration:** 4 seconds

**Camera:**
Close. We are at the base of the Heartwood looking up at Thrain. She is small against it. She is looking up at the stone above her — not at us. Her crystal staff is in her right hand, its tip resting on the ground. The staff hums faintly with the same note we just heard. Her expression is not dramatic. She looks like a person who has been standing here for a long time.

**What's in frame:**
Thrain from the waist up. The Heartwood behind and above her, violet light catching the edge of her robe. The note in her staff is visible as a faint glow at the crystal tip.

**Sound:**
A second voice joins the held note — higher, younger, warmer. The music has started, barely. Just strings, very quiet.

**SUPER ON SCREEN:**
```
THRAIN
The Keeper
```
*(Clean white text, bottom third. Fades in at 0:07, held through end of shot.)*

**THRAIN VOICE OVER:**
*(Soft. Young. Not self-pitying — she says it like a fact she has made peace with.)*

> *"The chord is mine to hold now.*
> *I have not been ready a single day since."*

**Direction note:** She does not sound afraid. She sounds honest. The second sentence carries the faintest trace of dark humour — not performed, just present. She has said this to herself before.
