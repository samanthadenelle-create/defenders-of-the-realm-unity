# Brand seed — the wall segment

> The logo should be built on a **wall segment** — because that one answer spawned everything.

## The origin
On 2026-05-30 the whole thing started with a single mundane question — *"why does this castle wall
look wrong?"* Chasing that one wall segment (its thickness, its scale, its nav) cracked open, in one
session, the entire architecture: the unified NavMesh, the visual⊥nav decouple, the catalog⊥repo
model, the generic dispatcher, the economy, the realm. **A humble wall segment was the seed of the
whole game.**

## Why it's the perfect symbol — it works on two levels
1. **Origin** — it's literally where everything began. Only the team knows the full story, but it's
   *the* symbol.
2. **Theme** — a wall segment **is the building block.** Everything in the game is built from
   segments: cells, defs, catalog parts, the realm itself. The logo *encodes the philosophy* — a
   realm built one segment at a time. The genesis and the gameplay are the same shape.

## Concept directions (for the art/brand pass)
- A single **low-poly, faceted stone wall segment** (matches the game's art direction = light + stylized).
- A **crenellated top** (merlons) reads instantly as castle / realm / defend.
- Could double as a letterform — a segment that forms the **"D"** in *Defenders*, or stacks into the title.
- The game's **dusk amber → violet palette** (LastChance lighting) ties the mark to the actual look.
- Tagline candidate: ***Defenders of the Realm — built one segment at a time.***

(A coding agent can't render the art — this is the seed + the meaning for whoever crafts the mark.)
