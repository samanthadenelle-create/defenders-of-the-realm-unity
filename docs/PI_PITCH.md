# Defenders of the Realm — Pi Network Flagship Pitch (one-pager)

> Draft distilled from `docs/NORTH_STAR.md`. The "show, don't tell" artifact — pair it with a polished
> playable build and it carries real weight in an ecosystem that's mostly vaporware.

---

## The hook
**Pi has 10 million users and almost nothing to *do* with their Pi.** It's a captive coin starving for
utility. We're the flagship game that gives them a place to **spend it, compete with it, and earn with it** —
and gives Pi the legitimate, polished, *fun* showcase its ecosystem has been missing.

## The problem (Pi's problem)
- Millions mined piles of Pi → **trapped, illiquid, no real utility.** Holders feel stuck.
- The ecosystem is **~60% low-effort apps.** Pi is fighting a "is this even real?" perception.
- Pi doesn't need *another* app. It needs a **flagship that proves the ecosystem is real.**

## The solution
**Defenders of the Realm** — a mobile competitive base-builder. **Clash of Clans × Warcraft:**
> Build your own stronghold → claim & defend resource nodes → raid other players → grow it even while you're away.

- **Pi becomes the in-game economy:** spend Pi on the loop, **buy into tournaments (e.g. 200 π, top-3 split the pot)**, earn it back. Real stakes, real utility, **real Pi velocity** in the ecosystem.
- **Evergreen by design:** a deep strategy + meta-counter engine means *years* of fresh competition from tiny content drips — sustained engagement, not a one-week spike.

## Why Pi (not the open market)
- **Distribution is the killer of indie games — Pi solves it.** A captive, mobile-first, **already-KYC'd** audience hungry for utility = pre-qualified players *and* a partly pre-solved compliance path for tournaments.
- **Quality is the moat.** In an ecosystem that's mostly junk, a genuinely polished game stands out disproportionately. We don't need to be the best game in the world — just the best **on Pi**.
- **We bring legitimacy, not ask a favor.** A real, polished flagship is **proof-of-life for the whole ecosystem.** That's value *for Pi.*

## Why us / why now (proof, not promise)
- A **working, polished playable build** — not slideware. Show it, don't pitch it.
- The full game + economy architecture **already exists** (combat, waves, base systems, multi-currency wallet rail, clans/chat) — built modular so **Pi integration is wiring, not a rebuild.**
- A clear **delivery ladder** (Tower → Town → Explore → Settle → Build-free) — shippable in playable steps, de-risked.

## The ask
A **flagship partnership**: featured placement + co-marketing to the Pi community, in exchange for the
quality, utility, and proof-of-life Pi's ecosystem needs. We bring the game; Pi brings the 10M.
