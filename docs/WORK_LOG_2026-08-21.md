# Work log — 2026-08-21

## Outcome

Today’s pass moved the live Android game toward a retention-first, satisfying loop across combat, dungeons, UI, economy, animation, and monetization. Two felt-test APKs were built and installed successfully on the connected Solana Seeker without changing the package ID or clearing player data.

## Gameplay and presentation

- Rebalanced mage mana so sustained casting exhausts MP instead of remaining effectively unlimited.
- Improved hero and enemy casting/locomotion animation coverage and refreshed late-bound animator parameters to stop sliding enemies.
- Added shared dungeon doors with reliable traversal and reusable room-builder support.
- Refined composed-dungeon size, challenge pacing, oil recovery, late-oil flicker, darkness, loot, and navigation behavior.
- Modernized hero selection/navigation UI and kept common management actions inline.
- Restored the founding-town choice and corrected structure orientation issues.
- Connected more catalogued VFX to spells and combat feedback where appropriate.

## Raids and armies

- Added a Tier-3 **Field Cleric troop** (not a Cleric hero) with formation healing and heal VFX.
- Made Shieldguards true tanks; defensive towers prioritize tanks before fragile allies.
- Activated the unlock bridge for the slow-moving Healing Caravan AoE support unit through Arcane Wellspring research.
- Capped runaway enemy contact-damage scaling while preserving difficulty through durability.
- Reduced unintended raid burst damage by ensuring decorative corner towers are not armed outside the authored DPS budget.
- Corrected sideways catapult placement by excluding siege silhouettes from the generic fallen-building rotation heuristic.
- Reduced first-clear crystal inflation and made replay raids pay reduced ordinary resources without becoming a crystal farm.
- Regenerated all four raid scenes and verified all four baked NavMeshes are walkable.

## Economy and monetization

- Reduced daily-quest and Crystal Mine crystal output and slowed rare-crystal spawning.
- Rebalanced build/upgrade timers, ad skips, grace windows, and rewarded-ad caps to preserve progression without making waiting punitive.
- Installed and resolved Unity LevelPlay Android dependencies.
- Added privacy/consent-before-initialization behavior and verified `LEVELPLAY_INIT_OK`.
- Configured rewarded placements for build skip, harvest boost, and daily chest; live rewarded fulfillment remains feature-gated until placement testing is complete.
- Completed whole-pack promo redemption through `PackCatalog.Find` and the same `PackStoreVM.ApplyPackContents` entitlement seam used by purchases. Older clients capability-fail without consuming pack codes.
- Curated the Realm Store into adventure, supplies, and realm-style sections; redundant legacy/high-ticket offers remain entitlement-compatible but no longer crowd the browsable shelf.
- Kept single-resource shortfall packs contextual instead of presenting a cash-for-resources wall.
- Added persisted convenience inventory fulfillment and introduced bounded Lantern Blessings: 2x or 3x oil duration for a fixed number of dungeon expeditions, consumed only when a run begins and never affecting combat or loot.

## Ember Deep device defect

- Captured the stuck state directly from the Seeker plus Android logcat.
- Proven root cause: the carried hero retained town coordinates and landed at approximately `(-1.85, -0.28, 144.68)`, embedded in portal/rock geometry. Input was not suppressed and the agent reported on-mesh.
- Added an explicit `HeroStartPoint_PlayerSpawn` landing contract to composed dungeon bakes.
- Rebuilt Ember Deep with a safe entry landing, no entry portal, no intermediate extract pads, and one exit portal in `warlord_keep` after the treasure/boss room.
- Verified the entry-to-Warlord NavMesh path is complete.
