## The one question every screen is reviewed against
**Does this screen give the player a reason to tap the NEXT one?** Not "is it pretty" - does it tie in.
A screen passes when a first-time player can answer, from the screen alone: what is this, what can I do
here, why would I, and where does that take me. A screen fails when it is a dead end, a no-op, a mystery
number, or a door to nothing (tonight's Troops toggle, the empty Research tab, `0m`, the aggregate
collectors line - all four were exactly this failure).
