# Defenders of the Realm: Echoes of Elarion

## Short Description (1 line)
A civilization gave their souls to survive. Now they need you to bring them back.

## Medium Description (store listing / itch.io)
When the World Ash fell, the people of Elarion had one choice left — imbue their souls into the last surviving root and wait for a hero brave enough to set them free. Centuries later, the Heartwood still pulses. The enemies never stopped coming. And the souls are still waiting.

Defenders of the Realm is a medieval fantasy village defense game where every wall you build, every tower you raise, and every wave you survive brings a dead civilization closer to rebirth. Defend the Heartwood. Free the trapped souls. Rebuild Elarion from the ashes — not through destiny, but through grit, determination, and partnership.

## Full Description

### The Story

Ten thousand years ago, the World Ash held the sky above the earth. Civilizations flourished in its shade. The people of Elarion built their lives in its light.

Then the Orc King Varuuk set the tree on fire.

The sky cracked. The branches fell. Every kingdom was buried in ash.

As the final walls crumbled and the enemy poured through, the people of Elarion made their last stand — not with swords, but with sacrifice. Every man, woman, and child pressed their hands to the last surviving root and gave it everything. Their souls. Their memories. Their hope.

They became the Echoes of Elarion — a civilization's spirit, sleeping inside a dying tree, waiting for someone brave enough to bring them back.

That someone is you.

### The Game

**Defend.** Build towers, fortify walls, and hold the line against relentless waves of orcs, goblins, and elemental horrors. Place Ballistae on your ramparts. Pour boiling oil from above your gates. Unlock the Heartwood's ancient powers as you prove yourself worthy.

**Rebuild.** Start with a broken village and a half-dead tree. Raise a Forge, an Arcane Tower, a Market — each one unlocked when you free a soul from the Heartwood. Every building has a purpose. Every NPC was once a person trapped inside the tree, now reborn because you defended long enough.

**Explore.** Venture beyond the gates into the Marches — dangerous lands rich with resources. Claim nodes, build outposts, and push deeper into forests, mountains, swamps, and volcanic wastes. Discover sealed dungeons left behind by the monks who once guarded the tree.

**Bond.** Tame spirit animals drawn to your dedication. The Grimhound, the Stonebear, the Starveil Stag — ancient creatures who follow those who refuse to give up. Craft offerings, earn their trust, and send them to gather resources or fight at your side.

**Grow.** Every wave survived makes the Heartwood stronger. Every soul freed adds life to the village. Watch Elarion transform from ruins to a thriving kingdom — not through magic, but through your refusal to let it die.

### What Are the Echoes?

The Echoes are not ghosts. They are not memories. They are the trapped souls of an entire civilization — thousands of people who chose to become part of the tree rather than disappear forever.

Every time you defend the Heartwood, it grows strong enough to release one more soul. The Blacksmith who picks up his hammer again. The Merchant who opens her stall. The Scholar who resumes her research. The Healer who tends the wounded.

They arrive grateful. They offer their spirit animals to help. And they ask one thing: keep going. Free the rest of their families.

You are not the chosen one. You are not fulfilling a prophecy. You are someone who looked at a decimated tree and a crumbling village and said: I will stay. I will rebuild. I will be the Echo of everything that was lost.

That is what Defenders of the Realm means. Not a title. A promise.

### Features

- **6 unique tower types** with 3-tier upgrade paths — Archery, Ballista, Flame, Catapult, Holy Shrine, and Boiling Oil Cauldrons
- **Wall-mounted defenses** — place Ballistae and Cauldrons directly on your ramparts
- **4 playable heroes** — Knight, Mage, Ranger, Cleric — each with a unique companion
- **Spirit animal taming** — craft offerings, bond with Echoes, assign them to defend or gather
- **Light crafting system** — Campfire, Herbal Mortar, Brewing Kettle progression
- **Procedural village growth** — buildings unlock as you free souls from the Heartwood
- **Overworld exploration** — 4 elemental zones with resource nodes, outposts, and dungeons
- **FF-style ATB party combat** — turn-based battles with party of 4
- **Tower defense mode** — standalone Defend the Spire with cinematic moments
- **Day/night cycle** — torch-lit nights, harder enemies after dark
- **Narrative dialogue** powered by Yarn Spinner — companion guides, NPC stories, branching choices
- **Mobile-first** — built for touch, plays great on desktop

But the Heartwood still pulses. The souls still wait. And now — you're here.

Rebuild the village. Defend the tree. Free the Echoes.

Not through power. Through persistence.
