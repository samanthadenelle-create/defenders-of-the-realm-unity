# Echo Social Layer — Vision (owner brainstorm 2026-06-14)

The prestige/emotional layer's SOCIAL surface. Echoes feel personal (your collection, the family thread —
Train Echo, Kayden's Big Boy 4014) AND make great social currency. Companion to the Echo Bond Shard system
(`WORK_ORDER_453_troops.md` → Echo Bond Shards). **Reward skill (3★) + time, never spending.**

## ⚠ SCOPE (architect's honest read — holds the "not an MMO / not before grant" line)
This is a **live-service social system**: most of it needs a **BACKEND** (leaderboards, gifting, borrowing,
guilds, trading, events) — it cannot be local. It is **post-grant**, and the deeper half (guilds, trading,
live-ops, NFTs) is far-future. It must NOT compete with the pre-listing critical path (the tight loop + the
troop thin slice). Captured as vision, deliberately deferred.

## The ONE near-term-worthy piece: Shareable Echo Cards
**Local, no backend, low-effort, and a real viral/marketing asset** — one-tap screenshot + watermark of a
bonded Echo with stats ("Kayden's Big Boy 4014 — Raid Legend"). Usable **pre-launch** to show the game off.
This is the only social piece worth considering near-term. The Echo **Gallery/profile** (bond % bars,
unlocked cosmetics, battle-history highlights) is also local (moderate effort) — a fast-follow candidate.

## Phased rollout (everything below the card = post-grant)
**MVP / near-term (local):** shareable Echo cards · Echo gallery/profile showcase.
**Phase 2 (post-grant, backend):** basic leaderboards (most Echoes bonded) · shard gifting (rate-limited,
common/rare only) · Echo "borrowing" (lend a bonded Echo for one friend's run) · guild Echo synergy.
**Phase 3 (far-future):** co-op raid challenges · themed collections ("Train Family", "Dragon Lineage") with
set bonuses · live-ops events ("Bond the Dragon Hatchling", top-100 rewards) · shard-only trading post (taxed)
· Echo Journal story-sharing to Discord/X · cosmetic skins/titles/flair · optional Solana cosmetic badges/NFTs.

## Anti-toxicity / fairness (baked in from the start)
All social features **opt-in** · **NO direct power trading** (gift/trade SHARDS only, never bonded Echoes) ·
rate limits on gifting/borrowing · privacy controls on collection visibility · **no pay-to-win** (shards are
skill-only; premium may speed training time, never buy shards).

## Why it fits (when its time comes)
Turns Echo-chasing into a social flex without pay-to-win; leverages the genuine emotional hook (the family
Echoes — see memory `family-echo-thread`); creates organic community; pairs with the Solana direction for
optional cosmetic badges later. The card alone is a marketing win at near-zero cost.
