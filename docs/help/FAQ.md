# Echoes of Elarion - Frequently Asked Questions

Quick, honest answers to the things players ask most. For the full walkthrough, see the
[Player Help Guide](HELP_GUIDE.md).

> Some answers point to features marked **(Coming Soon)** - those describe how a pillar is
> designed to work once it goes live. Anything not marked that way is playable right now.

---

### Why won't enemies spawn?

Because the **opening tutorial is still in progress.** The game deliberately holds the attackers
back until Sylas has walked you through placing your first defenses - it is giving you a quiet
moment to prepare, not a bug. Finish the tutorial (build your tower, then defend), and the waves
will begin. Once your realm is founded, enemies come in waves at your gates.

---

### How do I get more builders?

Each work channel (**Builders**, and later Training and Research) has a number of **active slots**
that set how many jobs can run at the same time. You can **buy an extra Builder slot** to run
more jobs in parallel. Extra slots are a **premium convenience** - like the classic city-builder
"extra builder's hut." Everything a builder does can also be sped up for free by **watching a
rewarded ad**, or finished **instantly**. Buying slots saves you *time*, never power.

---

### What happens to troops I lose in a raid? *(Coming Soon)*

Your troops are **wounded, not lost forever.** When an assault goes badly you lose some of the
troops you deployed, but they **recover over time** rather than being permanently destroyed.
Raiding is designed to be a repeatable habit, not a gamble with permanent loss.

*(Raiding and troops are in development and not yet playable - this is how the system is
designed to work when it goes live.)*

---

### Can I raid while I build? *(Coming Soon)*

Yes. Building and training run through **separate channels** (Builders vs. Training), so they
**never block each other** - a wall can upgrade at the same time your army trains. This is the
whole point of the multi-channel queue.

*(The Builders channel is fully live today; the Training channel comes online with the Raid
pillar.)*

---

### Do I need a wallet? Do I have to spend real money?

**No.** The game is **free and fully playable** with no wallet and no purchases - the entire core
loop (build, defend, explore, grow your Echoes) runs on in-game resources you earn by playing.

A wallet is **optional** and, on Solana Seeker / Android, does two things: it acts as your
**identity and cloud save** (your progress travels with your wallet), and it lets you **buy
optional packs in SKR** if you want to. Packs sell time and convenience, never power.

---

### How do stars work in a raid? *(Coming Soon)*

An assault is scored from **1 to 3 stars** based on how much of the enemy base you destroy - with
extra credit for razing the enemy Heart and for total destruction. More stars means a bigger
loot payout. It is the familiar "how clean was your victory" scoring from the city-builder genre.

*(Raiding is in development and not yet playable.)*

---

### What are Echoes?

**Echoes are your workforce and your quiet allies** - each one is the awakened essence of a soul
the Heart of Elarion guards (Aldwin, Elowen, Corvin, Bran, Doran, and Maren). They **never
fight**. You start with one, and awaken another every **5 waves** you clear, up to **six** total.

Each Echo has an element and a level, and can be assigned to a **lane**: **Harvest** (gathers
resources automatically), **Crafting** (helps you craft), **Defense** (boosts your whole city's
defenses), or **Exploration** (aids dungeon runs). Your first Echo auto-harvests for you from the
start - just remember to come back and tap **Dump All** to claim what it has gathered before its
silo fills up. *(Harvest and Crafting are the fully live lanes today; Defense and Exploration are
rolling out.)*

---

### How do I heal my hero?

A few ways:

- **Healing potions and food** from your **Consumables** inventory restore health, and can be
  used **mid-fight** - carry a couple before a hard battle.
- A **Tent / rest** heals you to full **between** fights.
- Your **mana** recovers on its own in safe areas.
- **Warden's Grace**, one of your hero skills, is a protective support cast for when a fight
  turns against you.

The most common way a run ends is running out of potions mid-fight, so stock up first.

---

### Is my progress saved?

**Yes.** Your progress saves **both on your device and to the cloud**, so you can close the app
or start a fresh session and pick up right where you left off. Your realm layout, your Echoes,
your resources, and your hero all persist.

---

### How do I grow faster / get more resources?

Three faucets, all working together:

- **Defend** - clearing waves rewards resources and awakens new Echoes.
- **Explore** - dungeons and open-world encounters drop loot.
- **Harvest** - assign Echoes to the **Harvest** lane and they gather for you around the clock,
  online and offline. More Harvest Echoes = a faster silo. Come back and **Dump All** to bank it.

Feeding **Grain** grows your **population**, which makes your workforce gather even faster - and
there is no downside, since Grain never drains or starves.

---

### Why did I lose my dungeon run when I lost a fight?

That is intended. Inside a dungeon, **losing a fight ends the run** and returns you to the
village - there is no free retry. Fight carefully, keep potions on hand, and remember the exit is
**always open** if you want to leave with what you have already earned rather than risk it.

---

### Do towers really shoot through walls?

**No** - and that is a deliberate rule. A **wall between a tower and its target blocks the shot**,
so when you lay out your defenses, make sure your towers have clear lines of fire to the gates
they are meant to cover.
