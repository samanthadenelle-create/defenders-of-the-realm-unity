# Echoes of Elarion - Player Help Guide

*Hold the last light.*

Welcome, Defender. This guide walks you through everything you can do in Elarion - founding
your realm, raising your walls, defending against the dark, delving the dungeons, and growing
your Echoes. Read it start to finish, or jump to the section you need. Nothing here spoils the
story; it only explains the systems.

> **A note on honesty:** this guide describes the game as it actually plays today. A few
> pillars are still being built. Anything not yet live is clearly marked **(Coming Soon)** so
> you always know what you can do right now versus what is on the way.

## 1. Getting Started - Founding Your Realm

When you first play, you choose your hero and **found your realm** at the **Heart of Elarion** -
the ancient world-tree at the center of your land. The Heart is the last light you are sworn to
hold; if it falls, your realm falls with it. Everything you build radiates out from it.

Your first minutes are guided by **Sylas**, a scout who meets you at the gate. He teaches you
the core loop by having you do it, not by making you read menus:

- **Move** to him.
- **Talk** to him.
- **Build your first tower.**
- **Defend the village** through your first wave of attackers.
- **Venture out** and win your first fight in the open world.
- **Return home**, where the Heart of Elarion answers your courage and your first **Echo**
  awakens.

That short arc *is* the game in miniature: **defend home, venture out, grow stronger, defend
better.** Once it is done, the world opens up and the pressure begins for real.

**Tip:** attackers will not come until the tutorial has taught you to place your defenses. This
is on purpose - the game gives you a moment of quiet to prepare. If you ever wonder "why isn't
anything spawning?", it means the opening tutorial is still in progress. Finish it and the waves
will begin.

## 2. Your Hero

You control **exactly one thing in the whole world: your Knight.** Everything else - towers,
walls, Echoes, workers - acts on its own. Your job is to be the hero on the field.

### Moving and the camera

- **Mobile:** drag the on-screen stick to move; the camera follows you.
- **Desktop (dev proxy):** WASD or the arrow keys.

### Fighting

Combat is **real-time**. You do not take turns - you position, dodge, and strike in the moment.
Your **action bar** sits on the right of the screen:

- **Attack** - your basic strike.
- **Four hero skills** - your Knight's kit: **Sword Wielding**, **Sword Heroic**, **Shield
  Charge**, **Warden's Grace**, and **Radiant Strike**. Skills use mana and have cooldowns, so
  spend them at the right moment rather than mashing.

**Warden's Grace** is a protective support cast - a good button to hit when a fight turns
against you.

### Healing your hero

Your Knight is not invincible, so keep yourself topped up:

- **Healing potions and food** (in your **Consumables** inventory) restore health, and can be
  used in the middle of a fight.
- A **Tent / rest** restores you to full **between** fights.
- In safe areas your mana recovers on its own.

Carry a couple of potions before a hard fight - running dry mid-battle is the most common way a
run ends badly.

## 3. Building & Upgrading

Elarion is **your** realm to shape. In **Build mode** you place, move, and rotate real,
functional structures wherever you like around the Heart.

### Building basics

- Open **Build mode** from the HUD.
- Pick a structure from the build palette.
- Place it on a valid spot - a ghost preview shows where it will go and whether the spot is
  allowed.
- Confirm with **PLACE**. On mobile, a small on-screen d-pad helps you nudge the preview into
  the exact spot before you commit.

You can build **defensive structures** (towers, walls, gates), **resource and production
buildings**, and more. Move or reposition anything you have placed as your plans change.

### Timers, the queues, and Builders

Big jobs take real time to finish - this is a build-and-return game, like the city-builders you
know. Work runs through **three parallel channels** so different kinds of jobs never block each
other:

| Channel | What it handles |
|---|---|
| **Builders** | Building, repairing, and upgrading your structures |
| **Training** *(coming online with troops)* | Training your army |
| **Research** *(coming online)* | Learning upgrades and unlocking new tiers |

Each channel has a number of **active slots** and its own waiting line:

- A job goes into a **free slot** and starts its timer.
- If every slot is busy, the job **queues** and starts automatically the moment a slot frees up
  - even while you are away.
- Because the channels are separate, a wall can be upgrading while (later) your army trains -
  they do not compete.

**Speeding things up:** you can **finish a job instantly**, or **watch a rewarded ad to knock
time off**. You can also **buy an extra Builder slot** so more jobs run at once. These sell you
*time and convenience* - never raw power.

> **What's live today:** the **Builders** channel is fully in play - build, repair, and upgrade
> right now. The **Training** and **Research** channels are wired and ready; their content
> (troops to train, research tracks to unlock) comes online with the Raid pillar (see
> [section 9](#9-raiding-coming-soon)).

### Repairing and rebuilding

Structures take damage in battle. **Repair** them through the Builders channel. If a structure
is fully **destroyed**, it does not come back for free - you build it again from scratch at full
cost, so protect what you raise.

## 4. Defending Your Village

This is the heart of the game: **hold the line at the Heart of Elarion.**

### How a wave works

Enemies attack in **waves**, marching on your **gates**. A wave is a burst of attackers; clear
it and you get a breather before the next. Between the towers you built and your own blade, you
hold the gates and keep the Heart safe.

- **Towers** fire on their own at enemies in range. Place them to cover your gates - and note
  that **a wall between a tower and its target will block the shot**, so plan your lines of fire.
- **Walls** slow and funnel attackers. Upgrade them to tougher tiers to buy your towers more
  time.
- **Gates** are where the pressure concentrates. Fortify every gate.
- **You** fight alongside your defenses - your hero is the flexible force that plugs whatever gap
  opens.

### Growing stronger through waves

Clearing waves is also how your realm grows: every **5 waves cleared** (four normal waves plus a
boss) awakens a new **Echo** (see [section 6](#6-echoes)). So defending is never just survival -
it is progress.

### The boss

Deep into the wave ladder, a great **dragon boss - Syndrath the Devourer** - descends: it flies
in, burns your towers, and turns on the Heart itself. Build up, upgrade your walls and towers,
and stock potions before you reach it.

## 5. The Dungeon

Beyond your walls lie **dungeons** - dark places to explore for lore and loot. The dungeon is a
full loop you can run end to end.

### Entering and moving

- Find a **dungeon door** in the world and interact with it to enter.
- Inside, move your hero through the rooms and corridors with the same controls you use in the
  world.

### What you do inside

- **Explore** the rooms - dungeons are laid out room by room, each with its own dressing and
  dangers.
- **Read lore stones** - walk up to a glowing stone and interact to read a fragment of Elarion's
  story.
- **Craft** - dungeons tie into crafting (for example, tending a light against the dark). Watch
  for crafting and checkpoint prompts as you go.
- **Fight** - encounters play out in **real-time combat**, the same as the open world. Win and
  you press on and claim your rewards; **lose and the run ends** and you return to the village.
  There is no free retry, so fight carefully.

### The boss and leaving

- Some dungeons hold a **boss** or keeper deeper in. Defeating it can open a back way through.
- There is **always an open exit** - you are never trapped. Use the **exit** interactable to
  leave whenever you like, and you will return to the village.

**Tip:** talk to **Bryn** and other dungeon dwellers when you meet them - they have things to
say, and sometimes to give.

## 6. Echoes

**Echoes are the soul of Elarion.** Each Echo is the awakened essence of one of the people the
Heart of Elarion guards - six named souls: **Aldwin, Elowen, Corvin, Bran, Doran, and Maren.**
They are not pets and they never fight for you. They are your workforce and your quiet allies.

### Collecting Echoes

- You begin with **one** Echo, awakened at the founding.
- Every **5 waves** you clear awakens another, up to a maximum of **six**.
- Each awakening is a short conversation that teaches you a little more about how your Echoes
  work together as your roster grows.

### Lanes - what your Echoes do

Every Echo has an **element** and a **level** (up to 8), and can be assigned to one **lane**:

| Lane | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Harvest** | Gathers resources for you automatically, online and offline |
| **Crafting** | Speeds and aids your crafting |
| **Defense** | A passive boost to your city's whole defensive package - the damage and health of your towers, walls, gates, and the Heart |
| **Exploration** | Aids your dungeon runs |

> **What's live today:** **Harvest** and **Crafting** are the lanes you will feel right now.
> **Defense** and **Exploration** are rolling out - assign them if you like, but their full
> effect is still coming online.

Your **very first Echo** is assigned to **Harvest** for you automatically, so you gather from the
start without doing anything. You learn to *choose* lanes once you have more than one Echo and
more than one lane matters.

### The Harvest loop - claim, don't hoard

A Harvesting Echo fills a shared **silo** over time - both while you play and while the app is
closed:

- The silo holds up to a **capacity measured in hours** (you can upgrade this).
- When it is full, it stops filling - so **come back and claim.**
- Tap **Dump All** to pour the silo into your main resource bins and reset the timer.

More Harvesting Echoes fill the silo faster. Growing your roster (by defending well) is what
grows your economy.

## 7. Currencies & Resources

Your realm runs on a small, honest set of resources:

- **Wood** - your everyday building material.
- **Iron** - for tougher structures and upgrades.
- **Grain** - feeds and **grows your population**, which in turn makes your workforce gather
  faster. Grain is pure growth: there is **no starvation and no upkeep** - you only ever gain.
- **Gold** - a flexible soft currency earned from battles.
- **Crystals** - a rarer resource for higher-tier upgrades.

You earn resources by **defending** (wave rewards), **exploring** (dungeon and encounter finds),
and letting your **Harvest Echoes** gather over time. You spend them on **building, upgrading,
and crafting.**

> **No crypto required to play.** The core game runs entirely on these in-game resources - you
> never need to spend real money to progress. See the next section for the optional store.

## 8. The Store, Your Wallet & Saving

### The store

Elarion offers optional **packs** in the store. The golden rule is **we sell time and
convenience, never power** - speedups, extra Builder slots, cosmetic and starter bundles - so a
paying player and a patient player both reach the same place. You can also **watch rewarded ads**
for free boosts.

Early-access packs are kept deliberately **cheap** (small tiers, with a low maximum). On the
Solana Seeker platform they are priced in **SKR**.

### Your wallet - identity and cloud save

If you play on **Solana Seeker / Android**, connecting a wallet gives you two things:

1. **Identity and cloud save** - your wallet is *who you are*. Your progress is tied to it, so
   you can pick up where you left off.
2. **A way to buy packs** in **SKR**, if you choose to.

**You do not need a wallet to play.** The game is free and fully playable without one; the wallet
simply adds cloud identity and the optional store on supported devices.

### Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your progress is saved **both on your device and to the cloud**, so a closed app or a fresh
session picks up where you left off. Your realm, your layout, your Echoes, and your hero all
persist between sessions.

## 9. Raiding (Coming Soon)

> **Status: in development.** Raiding is a full pillar we are building next. It is **not yet
> playable** - this section is a preview of how it is designed to work so you know what is ahead.
> Nothing below is live today.

The raid loop is built for players who know the city-builder genre: **train an army, choose a
target, deploy your war band, and watch the assault play out.**

1. **Train an army.** At the **Barracks** you will train troops - each costs resources and takes
   time, running through the **Training** channel (see [section 3](#3-building--upgrading))
   alongside your building, so you can build and train at once. Trained troops wait in your army
   housing up to a cap.
2. **Pick a target.** Open the raids screen and choose a difficulty card - each is a base to
   assault.
3. **Deploy on the field.** Tap to place your troops on the ring outside the enemy walls, then
   begin the assault.
4. **Watch it unfold.** You do not steer units mid-fight - you deploy and watch the auto-battle,
   the same real-time combat you already know.
5. **Earn your stars and loot.** Assaults are scored **1 to 3 stars** by how much you destroy
   (and whether you raze the enemy Heart), with a loot payout to match.
6. **Wounded troops recover.** Losing a fight costs you some of the troops you deployed - but
   your soldiers are **wounded, not gone forever.** They recover over time. Raiding is meant to
   be a habit, not a gamble with permanent loss.

Watch this space - when the Barracks and the raid screen go live, this section will move out of
"Coming Soon."
