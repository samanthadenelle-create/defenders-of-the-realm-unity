## 2. USPs / hooks (the reasons to care)

1. **The Echoes are guarded souls, not summons.** Your helpers are the awakened essences of the
   people your world-tree has kept through the dark -- six named souls, each with a story. Waking
   one is a moment, not a menu. (The emotional hook nobody else in the genre has.)

2. **Build a city that is actually yours.** Not a fixed base you decorate -- you place, move, and
   rotate every structure on your own land. The layout is your decision, on a phone, with your hands.

3. **Delve to forge.** Dungeons are not just fights -- they are where you pull the crafting
   ingredients that turn into your knight's next weapon and shield. Go down to come back stronger.

4. **One knight, real weight.** You control exactly one hero, Grom, in real-time arena combat.
   Every hit lands because you are watching it land -- no crowd to babysit, no party to micromanage.

5. **Reclaim the world, and watch it heal.** Pushing the dark back is persistent -- the tree
   brightens, more Echoes wake, the world visibly comes back to life while you play (and while you
   are away). Progress you can see.

6. **Crypto-native, and it respects you.** Built for the Solana Seeker and the Pi Browser --
   your audience, your rails. Optional rewarded ads and cheap cosmetic packs. Never pay-to-win.

7. **"Hold the last light."** A melancholy-hopeful tone the mobile RPG shelf does not have. Most
   games hand you a power trip; this one hands you something worth protecting.

## Appendix -- brand quick-rules (for whoever runs the campaigns)

- **Always** spell it "Echoes of Elarion" (chapter) in the "Defenders of the Realm" (series).
- **Tagline** is "Hold the last light." -- capital H, period, lowercase rest. Use it to close posts.
- **The hero** is Grom (a knight). Singular. Never "heroes" or "party."
- **The tree** is the Heart of Elarion (a world-tree / reliquary). Never "Avalon" (retired name).
- **Village is named Elarion** in-world. Never "Avalon."
- **Echoes never fight for you** -- they tend the world (harvest, craft, ward, scout). Don't imply
  they are battle summons.
- **Never** claim "play-to-earn," "pay-to-win," guaranteed returns, or crypto price talk. Monetization
  is optional cosmetics/convenience + rewarded ads.
- **Tone check:** if a line sounds like generic "epic adventure awaits" filler, cut it. The whole
  brand is the ONE warm light against the dark -- keep the melancholy-hopeful edge in every post.
```
