# Playtest Card — Build Mode (for Tricia) · 2026-06-01

**Build:** `Builds/Windows/DefendersOfTheRealm.exe` (double-click to run)

**What we fixed:** placing buildings in Build Mode wasn't working — when you tried
to place something, nothing showed up on the ground. That should be fixed now.

---

## What to try (about 2 minutes)

1. **Start the game** and get into the village.
2. **Open Build Mode** (the build/hammer button).
3. **Pick a building** from the menu that appears.
4. **Move your mouse over the ground.** ✅ You should now see a see-through
   "ghost" of the building that **follows your cursor**.
   - It turns **green** where you're allowed to build.
   - It turns **red** where you can't (too close to a gate, spot taken, etc.).
5. **Left-click on a green spot** → the building should be **placed** there.
6. Try **right-click** (or Esc) to cancel, and the **R key** to rotate the ghost
   before placing.
