Tutorial Flow Redesign — premium FTUE, wireframes + flow

Owner brief: flow feels amateurish; "childish buttons" highlighting the next action; verbiage is fine. Benchmark: 2026 FTUE practice (Supercell-class). The DATA-DRIVEN BONES STAY — tutorial-steps.json, TutorialFlow, TutorialSignals, the Onboarded gate — this redesigns the PRESENTATION + PACING layer only.
The four principles (from the research, each fixing a named complaint): 1. One focus — a dim mask with ONE lit cutout; the rest of the screen is dark and input-blocked (kills "what do I tap?"). 2. One elegant cue — a slim gold chevron that settles on the target, or a ghost-finger for gestures (replaces the childish boxed markers). 3. One line of story — the pet-Echo guide speaks one line per beat via the EXISTING dialogue portrait (teaching stays diegetic; no Next/Next cards). 4. One objective — a thin bottom strip with progress beads (replaces the fat top banner + its F8 collision, D2).
THE GUIDE IS YOUR FIRST PET — AN ECHO OF ELARION (owner re-ruling 2026-08-09, supersedes hero-rotation; rotation stays parked behind the identity seam). The KayKit "Sylas" stand-in is retired. The pet-Echo wakes near the Heart at ARRIVE (its essence returning is the cold open's payoff), leads the walk, speaks every guide line through the same dialogue portrait kit, and offers its own work at the ACK beat ("I can farm. I can mend. Put me to work, Keeper.") — introducing the game's namesake system in minute one. Never the player's own hero, by construction.

The flow — the owner's dynamic arc ("walk over here... grab this, move it... okay, great — let's move on")

0 Cold open cinematic (exists)→ 1 ARRIVE — the guide hero WALKS INTO frame by the Heart; one line of welcome→ 2 WALK — "come with me" — follow the guide to the gate (teaches joystick/camera by DOING, no text about controls)→ 3 BUILD ONE — one economy piece (spotlight Build, ghost-finger the drag) — "grab this... move it over here"→ 4 ACK — guide: "Good. You got it." (0.8s beat — the acknowledgment IS the pacing)→ 5 ONE CANNON — Defense tab, place ONE tower near the gate→ 6 TIMERS — spotlight the build timer: "work takes time — watch the ledger" (one line, no lecture)→ 7 ENEMIES AT THE GATE — a small scripted band hits the gate; player + tower repel it (the payoff)→ 8 WIN — quiet celebration; guide hands off: "The rest is yours, Keeper."
