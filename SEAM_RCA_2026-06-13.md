# SEAM RCA — Castle → OuterWorld south seam unreachable (2026-06-13)

**Status:** READ-ONLY RCA. No code changed.
**Scope:** Why the hero cannot cross the south (OuterWorld) seam from `MainCastle_Hall`,
and why the ruined/frost outpost connectors are likewise unreachable, while the troll
connector is reachable.

---

## 1. The proven data (live build SeamTrace, Player.log)

| Connector | Gate side (yaw) | `closestEver` | `radius` | Reachable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `WorldGate_ConnectToOuterWorld_Marker` (OuterWorld) | **South** (0°) | **34.4 m** | 12 m | NO |
| `OutpostConnector_Garrison_troll_outpost`           | **West** (90°)  | **1.4 m**  | 12 m | **YES** |
| `OutpostConnector_Garrison_ruined_keep`             | **North** (180°)| **35.9 m** | 12 m | NO |
| `OutpostConnector_Garrison_frost_keep`              | **East** (270°) | **34.8 m** | 12 m | NO |

The radius is 12 m, so the "Press F: Travel" prompt only appears once the hero is within
12 m. Three of four connectors never get closer than ~35 m, so their prompt never shows.

> **The headline pattern the prompt text obscures:** this is NOT "south is special."
> It is **"only the WEST gate is reachable; North, East AND South all stall ~35 m short."**
> One of four lanes works; three do not. That asymmetry is the whole diagnosis.

### Distance sanity check (proves it is NOT a straight-line distance problem)

- Spawn→South marker straight line = √(4.37² + 1.5² + 37.6²) ≈ **37.9 m**. Hero closed to
  34.4 m (walked ~3.5 m south) then stalled.
- Spawn→West(troll) marker straight line = √(34.6² + 1.5² + 4.37²) ≈ **34.9 m** — *farther
  in raw distance than the unreachable north/east connectors* — yet the hero reaches it

The hero physically traverses ~33 m of navmesh to reach the WEST connector (origin →
x −34.6) but cannot traverse the equivalent ~35 m on the South/North/East radials.

## 3. Verdict: NAVMESH EDGE / BAKE asymmetry, NOT an invisible collider

**It is (a) the navmesh walkable surface ending ~35 m out on three of the four radials —
not (b) a discrete invisible wall and not (c) a mis-placed marker.** Evidence:

1. **The hero is a `NavMeshAgent`-driven mover.** `HeroLocomotion` Awake() adds a
   `NavMeshAgent` and calls `_agent.Move(step)` while on-mesh (MASTER_CATALOG §2c, the
   "pure transform" header is a documented lie). A NavMeshAgent **stops at the navmesh
   edge** regardless of colliders — so "can't reach" almost always = bake, not geometry.
   `SceneTransitionTrigger`'s own class header says exactly this (`SceneTransitionTrigger.cs:16-20`:
   "A NavMeshAgent hero stops at the castle navmesh EDGE and can't physically reach a
   trigger box just beyond it").

2. **The asymmetry rules out a uniform blocker.** The builder places the inner ring, gate
   strips and seam-fill with x4 rotational symmetry (`BuildGateExitStrips` /
   `BuildInnerWallRing` rotate the SAME south authored values by 90/180/270 around origin,
   `CastleHubBuilder.cs:715-723, 481-503`). If a *collider* were the cause it would block
   all four sides equally. Instead exactly one side (West) bakes through and three do not —
   the fingerprint of a **fragmented/eroded bake**, not a wall.

3. **The stall radius matches the inner ring, not the spawn ring.** closestEver ≈ 34-36 m
   from markers at ~38 m out means the hero stops ~2-4 m short of the gate mouth, i.e. at
   the outer lip of the gate exit strip. The CourtyardFloor_Nav is authored 130×130 m
   (±65 m, `CastleHubBuilder.cs:655`) and the gate strips reach 18 m out
   (`outsideReach = 18f`, `CastleHubBuilder.cs:737`) — so the *intended* surface easily
   spans past every gate. The hero stopping short means the bake did **not** realize that
   intended surface on 3 of 4 radials (gate-arch voxelization eating the lane, agent-radius
   erosion at the inner-ring gap, or the strip baking as an island not fused to the
   courtyard sheet — all bake artifacts the builder's own comments fight at lines 727-734,
   766-771).

4. **No moat / discrete south wall exists to be the "invisible wall."** Grep of the builder
   for `moat` → none. The only south geometry is the recipe wall segments + `CornerTower_South`
   (`castle-south-recipe.json:1`) and the inner ring — all present symmetrically on every side.
   The owner's checklist phrase "invisible walls near south gate" is, on this evidence, the
   **navmesh edge** being felt as a wall (the agent stopping), not a stray collider.

> **One caveat I cannot close from static files:** the actual baked NavMesh data is not
> human-readable here. The above is inferred from positions + the agent model + the 1-of-4
> reachability fingerprint. A headless `[Flow:*]` play capture that logs
> `NavMesh.CalculatePath(spawn → each marker)` status (Complete/Partial/Invalid) would
> *prove* edge-vs-blocker per radial in minutes (CLAUDE.md §12). Recommend running it before
> the bake-side fix in Option C.

## 4. Ranked fix recommendation

### ★ Recommended: **Option A now (unblock the player) + Option C next (root cause).**

Because `requireConfirm = true` (`SceneTransitionTrigger.cs:45`) the old auto-fade
game-breaker is gone — a wider radius only makes the **prompt** reachable; it never
teleports without an explicit F/tap. So widening is safe *and* is the fastest unblock. But
widening alone leaves the hero pressing F from 35 m away through a wall he can see — the
**proper** fix is to make the navmesh actually reach the gate (Option C). Do A to ship, C
to be right. This is the "what is right, not what is easy" split, named explicitly.

---

### Option A — widen the three stalled seam radii to clear the navmesh edge (TACTICAL)

Required radius = `closestEver + margin`. Use **38 m** (covers 35.9 m worst case + ~2 m).

**Exact edits (all in `Assets/Editor/CastleHubBuilder.cs`; values set via reflection):**

1. **South (OuterWorld) seam** — `EnsureExitSeamAtRecipeGate`, **line 1784**:
   ```
   transType.GetField("ProximityRadius")?.SetValue(comp, 12f);
   ```
   change `12f` → **`38f`** (closestEver 34.4 + margin).

2. **Outpost connectors (ruined/frost — and troll harmlessly)** — `WireOutpostConnectors`,
   **line 1173**:
   ```
   if (fRadius != null) fRadius.SetValue(comp, 12f);
   ```
   change `12f` → **`38f`** (closestEver 35.9 worst case + margin). Troll already fires at
   1.4 m; 38 m doesn't hurt it (confirm-to-cross, no auto fire).

> These are BAKE-TIME values. The change only takes effect when CastleHubBuilder re-runs
> against `MainCastle_Hall`. **But §18 of MASTER_CATALOG warns a CastleHubBuilder regen
> REVERTS the owner's hand-dialed offsets** — so a full rebuild is NOT free. Two safe ways
> to apply: (i) a targeted editor one-shot that only finds the four
> `SceneTransitionTrigger`s and sets `ProximityRadius`, then saves the scene (no geometry
> regen); or (ii) the owner edits the four `ProximityRadius` fields in the Inspector. Do
> NOT trigger a geometry rebuild just to change a radius.

**Box collider note:** the trigger `BoxCollider.size` (12×6×10, scene line 7197 / builder
1164,1788) is only used by the OnTriggerEnter fallback, which `requireConfirm` short-circuits
(`SceneTransitionTrigger.cs:149`). The live path is the proximity sphere (`ProximityRadius`),
so the box size is irrelevant to this fix — only the radius matters.

#### Option A risk: multi-prompt overlap near the spawn ring — REAL, assess before shipping 38 m

`MobileInteractButton` is a **single shared button** that shows exactly ONE request per
frame (`MobileInteractButton.cs:104-138`): highest `priority` wins, ties → first-come.
**Every seam calls `Request(this, …, …)` with the default priority 0**
(`SceneTransitionTrigger.cs:116`). Consequences when two+ seams are simultaneously in range:

- **Touch (mobile):** only ONE prompt label shows; it flickers between the competing seams
  frame-to-frame (non-deterministic first-come at equal priority). The player can still tap,
  but may cross to whichever seam happens to own the button that frame — confusing, not
  fatal.
- **Desktop (F key):** WORSE. Each `SceneTransitionTrigger.Update` independently checks
  `Input.GetKeyDown(KeyCode.F)` while in range (`SceneTransitionTrigger.cs:118`) and calls
  its own `Cross()`. Two overlapping seams both see F the same frame → **both fire Cross()**.
  `_fired` guards re-entry per-trigger but does not coordinate across triggers, so the second
  `LoadScene` call can stomp the first. This is the exact class of bug the radius-12 comment
  (`CastleHubBuilder.cs:1170-1172, 1781-1783`) was guarding against.

**Geometry check — do 38 m radii overlap at spawn?** Spawn = origin. Marker distances from
spawn: South 37.9 m, West 34.9 m, North/East ~38 m (symmetric). A 38 m radius around a
marker ~38 m from origin **reaches the origin** — so at the spawn point, potentially ALL
FOUR prompts are simultaneously in range. That re-creates the "4 gates surround the ~35 m
spawn" hazard the owner already hit once, except now it's prompt-flicker + the desktop
double-Cross, not an auto-fade.

**Mitigations if you ship Option A at 38 m:**
- Prefer a **tighter radius**: the hero only reaches ~34-36 m out, so **36 m** (not 38)
  still fires at the edge while keeping the origin (37.9 m away on south) just OUTSIDE the
  south sphere. But north/east at ~38 m would still include origin — so radius alone can't
  fully separate them.
- Give the seams a **priority** + an explicit "nearest seam wins" rule, OR have
  `SceneTransitionTrigger` only `Request`/accept F when it is the CLOSEST in-range seam to
  the hero. (Small code change in `SceneTransitionTrigger`; out of scope for this RCA but
  the clean way to make Option A safe.)
- This overlap risk is the strongest argument for doing **Option C** instead: if the
  navmesh actually reaches each gate, radius stays at 12 m (fires only AT the gate) and the
  four spheres never overlap at the spawn.

### Option B — move the markers inward to where the hero can stand (REJECTED as primary)

You *could* set each marker to the hero's actual closestEver point (e.g. south marker
z from -37.6 → ~ -3, ruined/frost similarly ~35 m inward). Pose change would be in
`EnsureExitSeamAtRecipeGate:1753` and `WireOutpostConnectors:1154`.

**Rejected because** it puts the "Travel to the Outer World" prompt 35 m INSIDE the
courtyard, nowhere near the visible gate — the player would cross to OuterWorld by pressing
F in the middle of their own courtyard. It hides the symptom (prompt unreachable) while
making the UX nonsensical and leaving the real navmesh hole. Only sane as a stopgap if A is
somehow unavailable.

---

### Option C — fix the navmesh so the lane actually reaches all four gates (CORRECT root cause)

The real defect: three of four gate lanes don't bake into a continuous walkable surface
from spawn to gate mouth (proven by West-reachable / N-E-S-stalled). Fix the bake so the
hero can walk to within 12 m of every gate, then radius stays 12 m and the overlap risk in
Option A evaporates.

Candidate causes to verify in a headless `NavMesh.CalculatePath` capture (do this FIRST):
- **Gate-arch voxelization sealing 3 of 4 lanes.** `ExcludeGatesFromNavBake`
  (`CastleHubBuilder.cs:669`) is meant to drop the arch meshes from the bake; verify it
  actually excludes all four gates' arches, not just one. If West is the authored side and
  the exclusion is keyed to the authored gate name, the rotated W/N/E copies may not be
  excluded — that would explain one-side-works.
- **Inner-ring gap not baking passable on 3 sides.** Gaps are centered on
  `gateLateral = ReadSouthGatePos().x = -4.37` for ALL sides
  (`CastleHubBuilder.cs:478,499-502`). The comment claims x4 symmetry makes this correct,
  but if the wall RUN scaling/placement (`BuildRingSideRun:522`) leaves a sub-agent-radius
  sliver on the rotated sides, the gap bakes closed. Verify each side's gap is ≥ 2×
  agent radius (0.4) wide after erosion.
- **Gate exit strips baking as islands.** Strips are forced walkable
  (`AddWalkableNavMeshModifier`, `CastleHubBuilder.cs:771`) but if a rotated strip doesn't
  physically overlap the courtyard sheet it bakes as a disconnected island the agent can't
  step onto. West may overlap; the others may sit just off the sheet.

Fixing whichever the capture indicts is the durable fix. It is a CastleHubBuilder geometry
change → re-bake → and must be reconciled with the owner's hand-dialed scene (§18) — i.e. a
work order for CLI, not a radius poke.

## 5. Summary

- **Spawn = origin (0,0,0)** (`MainCastle_Hall.unity:8609`); seam markers ~38 m out on each
  radial.
- **Verdict: navmesh edge / fragmented bake, NOT an invisible collider** — proven by the
  1-of-4 reachability fingerprint (West reaches 1.4 m; N/E/S all stall ~35 m) and the
  NavMeshAgent stop-at-edge model.
- **Ship now:** Option A — raise the three stalled `ProximityRadius` 12 → **38** at
  `CastleHubBuilder.cs:1784` (south) and `:1173` (outposts), applied via a targeted
  scene-only edit, NOT a geometry regen (§18).
- **Overlap risk is real:** at 38 m the spawn at origin can be in range of all four seams;
  `MobileInteractButton` shows one prompt (flicker) and desktop F can double-fire `Cross()`
  across two triggers. Mitigate with a "nearest in-range seam wins" rule in
  `SceneTransitionTrigger`, or prefer Option C.
- **Be right:** Option C — make the bake reach all four gates so radius can stay 12 m and
  the spheres never overlap. Run a headless `NavMesh.CalculatePath(spawn→marker)` capture
  per radial first to pin which of {arch exclusion, inner-gap erosion, strip island} kills
  N/E/S.
