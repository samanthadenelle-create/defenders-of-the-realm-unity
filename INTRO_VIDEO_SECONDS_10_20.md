# Defenders of the Realm — Intro Video
## Seconds 10–20: Shot Direction + Voice Over

---

## SHOT 3 — GROM (0:10 – 0:16)

**Duration:** 6 seconds

**Camera:**
Medium shot. Grom is standing at the top of the wall, facing outward into the dark. Camera is behind and slightly below him — we see his back, the armour, and the dark valley stretching out ahead. Hold for 2 seconds on his back. Then cut to a front-facing close-up — we see his face for the first time. He is not scanning, not worried. He is simply watching. He looks like a man who has been here before.

**What's in frame:**
Shot 1: Grom's back, the dark beyond the wall, the Heartwood's violet glow reflected faintly on his pauldrons.
Shot 2 (cut at 0:12): His face. Older. Set. The banner-fittings worked into his armour are visible at the shoulder — repurposed, not ceremonial. The Heartwood is behind him in soft focus.

**Sound:**
The strings from Thrain's shot continue. A low, slow percussion enters on the cut to his face — not a drum roll, just a single low hit, like a door closing.

**SUPER ON SCREEN:**
```
GROM
The Last Banner
```
*(Same style as Thrain. Appears on the cut to his face at 0:12, held through end of shot.)*

**GROM VOICE OVER:**
*(Deep. Even. He does not raise his voice. Not performing resolve — he just has it.)*

> *"I made a promise to a tree that was already dead.*
> *I haven't broken a promise yet."*

**Direction note:** The slight pause between the two sentences is intentional. The first sentence should sound almost wry — not bitter, but aware of how it sounds. The second sentence is the whole man in one line. He means it completely.

## SHOT 4 — SYLAS (0:16 – 0:21)

**Duration:** 5 seconds

**Camera:**
No slow build here — we cut straight into motion. Sylas is moving fast along the outer path at the base of the wall, low light, bow in hand. Camera tracks alongside her at pace, handheld-feel. She doesn't look at us. She is checking the dark beyond the treeline with quick, practiced glances. At 0:19, she stops briefly, reads something in the dark — we don't see what — and keeps moving.

**What's in frame:**
Sylas in motion. The treeline at the edge of the village, dark and deep. The faint sound of the Choir counter-note is here — just barely audible under the music, like a hum from the wrong direction.

**Sound:**
The percussion from Grom's shot picks up slightly into a rhythm that matches Sylas's pace. Not dramatic — just moving.

**SUPER ON SCREEN:**
```
SYLAS
The Outer Paths
```
*(Appears at 0:16 as she enters frame. Faster fade-in than the others — matches her energy.)*

**SYLAS VOICE OVER:**
*(Dry. Fast. Alert. The tone of someone filing a report.)*

> *"I've been marking them on the roads for ten years.*
> *There are a lot more of them now."*

**Direction note:** Both lines are delivered at the same pace — no dramatic drop on the second line. The fact that it's deadpan is what makes it land. She's not scared. She's counting.
