### The asymmetry this sweep is built on

A wrongly-closed ticket loses real work **silently**; a wrongly-kept one costs one line of noise. So `AGED-UNVERIFIED` is the DEFAULT for anything not positively placed, and it **keeps its READY status** — 35 of 35 READY work orders are left on the board untouched or note-only.
