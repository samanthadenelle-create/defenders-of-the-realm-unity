## Full script — 22 seconds

---

### OPENING (0:00 – 0:06)

**SHOT:**
Wide. The village square of Elarion at dusk. The Heartwood fills the centre of frame — the ancient Heart-Tree, regrowing. Massive twisted trunk, bark pale and veined with warm light. Lanterns hang from the branches, swaying. Green leaves at the crown catching the last light. The stone well-ring around its roots. The mosaic plaza circle glowing faintly beneath. The village buildings ring it on all sides, small against the tree's height. The dark pressing in at the frame's edges. Four figures — Sylas, Grom, Elara, Thrain — walking toward the Heartwood from across the plaza. As each passes the camera, their name appears on screen.

**SUPERS** *(each fades in as the hero passes, holds 1.5 seconds)*:
`SYLAS — The Elven Ranger`
`SIR GROM — The Knight`
`ELARA — Divine Healer`
`THRAIN — Grand Wizard`

**SOUND:**
Low, single held note. The Heartwood's chord — organic, resonant through wood and root. No music. Just the note.

**NARRATOR V.O.** *(warm, unhurried)*
> *"The Heart-Tree burned a hundred winters ago.*
> *The Folk kept its roots alive — and after a hundred winters, it is coming back."*

---

### HERO 1 — THRAIN (0:06 – 0:10)

**SHOT:**
Close on Thrain at the base of the Heartwood, looking up at the canopy. Crystal staff resting on the mosaic ground. Grey-haired, battle-worn, robes frayed. He looks like a man who has been holding something together for a very long time.

**SUPER:** `THRAIN — Grand Wizard`

Strings enter, very quiet.

**THRAIN V.O.** *(gravelled, matter-of-fact)*
> *"The chord is mine to hold now.*
> *I have not been ready a single day since."*

---

### HERO 2 — GROM (0:10 – 0:15)

**SHOT:**
First: Grom's back at the top of the wall, facing the dark. Then cut to his face — set, watchful, not worried. Low percussion hits on the cut.

**SUPER:** `SIR GROM — The Last Banner`

**GROM V.O.** *(deep, even — wry on the first line)*
> *"I made a promise to a tree that was already dead.*
> *Turns out it wasn't."*

---

### HERO 3 — SYLAS (0:15 – 0:19)

**SHOT:**
Straight into motion — Sylas moving fast along the outer path, bow in hand. He stops briefly at 0:17, reads something in the treeline, keeps moving.

**SUPER:** `SYLAS — The Outer Paths`

Percussion picks up to match his pace.

**SYLAS V.O.** *(dry, deadpan)*
> *"I've been marking them on the roads for ten years.*
> *There are a lot more of them now."*

---

### HERO 4 — ELARA (0:19 – 0:22)

**SHOT:**
Elara kneeling beside a wounded villager near the gates, working fast. She glances up at the Heartwood once — the tree, alive and glowing — then back down.

**SUPER:** `ELARA — Divine Healer`

Music drops back slightly, warmth over intensity.

**ELARA V.O.** *(warm, matter-of-fact)*
> *"Someone has to keep everyone standing.*
> *It might as well be me."*


---

### CLOSING (0:22 – 0:28 with title hold)

**SHOT:**
Pull back wide. All four visible as small figures — the Heartwood above them all, amber-warm light in the bark and branches. At the frame's edge, in the dark beyond the wall, something moves. We don't hold on it.

**NARRATOR V.O.** *(quieter)*
> *"The note holds.*
> *For now."*

**TITLE CARD:** `DEFENDERS OF THE REALM` — fades in, held 2 seconds. Music peaks, cuts clean.

## Voice direction — full brief

**Narrator:** Warm, old-world, unhurried. Not epic-trailer bombast. The urgency is in the words, not the delivery.

**Thrain:** Older male voice. Gravelled, not dramatic. He says it like a fact he made peace with a long time ago. The weight is real but he's not performing it.

**Grom:** Low and even. The line "Turns out it wasn't" should land quiet and almost warm — he's not triumphant, he's just glad. It's the most human line in the script.

**Sylas:** Fastest of the four, male. Alert and dry. "A lot more of them now" is deadpan — not scared, just counting.

**Elara:** Warmest read, no sentimentality. "It might as well be me" is competence, not resignation.

---

## Music direction

**0:00 – 0:06:** Single held note only. No underscore.
**0:06 – 0:10:** Strings enter very quietly under Thrain.
**0:10 – 0:19:** Gradual build. Low percussion on Grom. Rhythm picks up on Sylas to match his pace.
**0:19 – 0:22:** Drops back for Elara — warmth over intensity.
**0:22+:** Swell to peak on wide shot + title. Cut clean on the last beat.

---

## Production notes

- The Heartwood's amber-warm light is the visual through-line — visible or felt in every shot
- Grom's new line ("Turns out it wasn't") is the emotional pivot of the whole script — the tree he promised to protect is alive again. Give it room.
- Supers in the opening use the hero card style from character select
- All hero shots: caught in the middle of doing the thing they do — not poses
