## MONEY - READ THIS BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE

**No step in the main lap asks you to spend a single SKR.** You have already paid twice out of your
own wallet to test this rail. The two money-shaped changes in this build - the post-purchase
processing screen (WO-1188) and paid overflow (WO-1191) - are both written below so you can judge
them **without buying anything**:

- **Overflow** is reachable through **Dev tools -> "Load resources (full base)"**, which grants
  **uncapped** - it puts you above the cap for free. Row T4.
- **Post-purchase** is reachable in its **reconcile** shape, which by construction makes **no new
  payment**. Row S4.

Anything that genuinely cannot be judged without a real charge is in **the last section**, with its
price written next to it, and it is entirely optional. Our recommendation there is: do not buy.

## What is NOT proven, and how it will bite you

- **`MAINNET_SALES_ENABLED` is UNTESTED.** `walletAllowed` passes **your** wallet unconditionally,
  *before* the switch is ever consulted, and both settled purchases so far were yours. **Your store
  working proves nothing about what a stranger sees.** If the switch is off, a real player gets no
  error at all - every card reads "Price unavailable". Only a price-list call from a wallet that is
  not yours settles it, and that is not something you can do from this device.
- **The `api/` server half of WO-1190 is not deployed.** The APK now asks for prices **without**
  attaching a signature; the server currently live still expects one. So the likely outcome today is
  **no wallet prompt (the fix working) AND "Price unavailable" on every card (the server half
  missing)**. That combination is the expected half-shipped state, not a new defect. See rows S1/S2.
- **WO-1179's thresholds are guesses in the source** - waves 5 and 10 were chosen with no authored
  tuning behind them, and the `waves.json` enemy batches are inert. Read rows W2/W3 before judging.
- **WO-1191's exact sentences are a proposal.** The behaviour is what is being asserted; the words
  are yours to overrule.

# The lap

## 0. Boot / first load

**Look for:** enemies as real art and buildings as real models.
**Fail tell:** tinted **capsules** and placeholder buildings, with **no error anywhere on screen**.
That means this build's content bundles never reached the CDN. It has gone wrong three times before
and it never announces itself - if you see capsules, **stop the lap and say so**, because every later
row would be judged against the wrong build.

## Needs state you may not have - these waste a sitting if hit cold

- **S4 / S5 (WO-1188)** - a **pending, already-paid purchase on record**. With none, the processing
  screen cannot be shown without spending, and it should not be.
- **W2 / W3 (WO-1179)** - a town that survives to **wave 5** and then **wave 10**. By far the
  longest-running rows here. Do them last, or in their own sitting.
- **T2 (WO-1191, at-cap branch)** - a resource actually **at** its cap with a collector still filling.
- **T4 / T5 (WO-1191, over-cap branch)** - **Dev tools must open on the device**.
- **G1 (WO-814)** - an **owned** weapon that is not already at max level, plus a vendor open.
- **S3 (WO-1190)** - a SKU the server marks unsellable. With the server half undeployed there may be
  no such row today.

## Partially shipped - do not fail these for the missing half

- **WO-1190** - the **server half is not deployed**. "No prompt + no prices" is the expected
  half-shipped state. Judge the prompt and the prices as **two separate answers**.
- **WO-1189** - **only the overlap is in scope.** The panel as a whole went back to the UI seat as
  **WO-1192**: portrait has an empty parchment slab (it will hold the quest illustration), quest
  titles truncate to "Standing Wa...", the Daily tab is sheared, objective text clips mid-word, and
  landscape leaves the bottom ~40% empty with Close floating in it. **All of that is already known
  and already ticketed.** The only question in T3 is whether Accept still sits on the status line.
- **WO-814** - the machinery is complete and **deliberately silent**. Empty is the shipped state, not
  a failure. It stays silent until you author the ability names.
- **WO-1191** - the mechanics were already correct; what shipped is the **wording**. Treat T4 as a
  copy review as much as a bug check.
- **WO-1186** - chips resolving under the minimum touch size (B4) was **flagged and deliberately not
  fixed**. It is an open decision, not an escaped defect.
- **WO-1179** - only the arrival side changed. **No breach consequence is wired** - nothing new
  happens when a side gets through. Do not fail it for that.
