## 0. SUCCESS CRITERIA — set BEFORE reading results (avoid confirmation bias)
We measure **demand signals, NOT vanity metrics.** Upvotes/likes are noise; what matters is people *asking for
the solution.*

| Signal tier | What it looks like | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **STRONG (build it)** | ≥3–5 unsolicited "how do I get this / tell me more / DM me / where's the waitlist" per post; people describing their OWN pain in detail; saves/shares; any "I'd pay for this" | Real demand. Stand up the waitlist + playbook fast. |
| 🟡 **MODERATE (refine)** | Lots of "yes this is my problem" resonance but NO ask-for-solution; high engagement, low intent | Pain is real, offer/monetization unproven. Add a soft CTA / lead magnet, re-test. |
| 🟠 **NICHE (narrow)** | Strong resonance from ONE specific group (e.g. non-coder builders, PMs, solo founders) only | Niche down the message + product to that buyer. |
| 🔴 **WEAK (iterate or drop)** | Crickets, "this is just X", "free tools do this", no resonance | Wrong message/channel/angle — iterate before concluding. |

**The one number that matters:** count of **"I want this / tell me more"** asks across all posts. That's the demand.

## 5. DECISION FRAMEWORK — what we build next, by feedback pattern
- 🟢 **Strong demand** → (1) stand up a waitlist landing (Carrd/Notion + email), (2) I draft the FULL teardown
  post with real stories from our logs, (3) outline the first small paid cohort. Move fast while it's hot.
- 🧩 **"Show me it actually works / proof?"** → lead with the receipts: a concrete CASE-STUDY post using THIS
  game + the live itch build as proof ("built this way, here's the before/after"). Proof converts skeptics.
- 🎁 **"I want the system" but no pay intent** → free **mini-playbook lead magnet** (1–2 of the 7 guardrails) to
  capture emails + warm them toward the paid cohort. Tests willingness without giving away the whole thing.
- 🎯 **One segment lights up** (e.g. non-coder builders / PMs / solo founders) → re-write the message + the
  product squarely for THAT buyer; they're the beachhead.
- 🗣️ **"This is just prompt engineering / free tools do this"** → sharpen the differentiator: PM discipline
  (not prompting) + a shipping game (not a toy) + your 20-yr credibility. Address it head-on in the next post.
- 🔴 **Crickets** → it's the message or the channel, not necessarily the idea. Iterate the hook/title, try a
  different sub/format (a story, a screenshot of the chaos, a before/after) before drawing conclusions.
