About

The Heart still stands

The Heart of Elarion still stands, and it still remembers. Raise the last village around it, wake the Echoes sleeping in its roots, and hold the dark back one night at a time.

Echoes of Elarion is a town-builder and defense game. You lay out your own village around the Heart — the world tree at its centre — place and upgrade its buildings, walls and towers, keep the builder, training and research queues turning, and defend it when the waves come. Between defenses you range out across the Realm Map to raid what the dark has taken.

The Echoes are the awakened essences of the people the Heart once guarded. Six of them wake across a run — Aldwin, Elowen, Corvin, Bran, Doran and Maren — and each returns with a calling of its own. You decide what each one tends: match an Echo to its calling and it gives back more, but the choice stays yours and is never locked.

Under the realm there are the dungeons. Six of them open through a portal in the 2026-09-07 build, plus one crossroads that is not a dungeon, and the rough stones you carry back out are what the Jeweler turns into rings of power.

The 2026-09-07 update

What changed this week

Built by one studio and patched daily. Every line below ships with the commit that made it.

  • I

    Sign in once

    The game remembers you for the day. Your wallet is asked for only when you buy something or redeem a code — never just to open the game.

  • II

    Manage, rebuilt

    Nine pages that fill the screen, buildings as picture cards, a research tree, a queue drawer for what is building, training and researching, and a way out of every page.

  • III

    Raids that read

    Every camp says what it pays before you commit. Raiders take jobs — a front line, skirmishers, breakers and support — and dying no longer ends the raid: your army fights on.

  • IV

    Time, not gold

    Training and upgrading troops costs time; gold only skips a wait or hires reinforcements. Stone has replaced Food across the realm and is mined at the new Quarry.

Echoes of Elarion is built and shipped by one studio, and it is patched daily. If the game does not do what the screen says it will, the flag button in the corner captures the frame and the recent diagnostic trace and sends it straight to the team, so a defect arrives as evidence instead of a description. Cloud saves are keyed to your wallet address and a wallet-keyed save needs a signature from that wallet over a single-use challenge, so nobody else can write to your town; guests get a backup too, under an opaque device-derived identifier. Packs are paid in SKR through your own wallet, which shows the token, the exact amount, the network and the recipient before you approve anything, and the dApp Store takes no cut — on a one-person budget that is the difference between a hobby and a roadmap. SKR is Solana Mobile's token, not ours: we have never minted a token and never will, and there is no airdrop, no trading, no secondary market and no cash-out. Everything you buy is non-transferable, stays inside your account, and is never needed to finish the game.
