About

The Heart still stands

The Heart of Elarion still stands, and it still remembers. Raise the last village around it, wake the Echoes sleeping in its roots, and hold the dark back one night at a time.

Echoes of Elarion is a town-builder and defense game. You lay out your own village around the Heart — the world tree at its centre — place and upgrade its buildings, walls and towers, keep the builder, training and research queues turning, and defend it when the waves come. Between defenses you range out across the Realm Map to raid what the dark has taken.

The Echoes are the awakened essences of the people the Heart once guarded. Each returns with a calling of its own, and you decide what each one tends — match an Echo to its calling and it gives back double.

What you do

Four pillars of the night

  • I

    Build the village you want

    Place every building yourself, move it later, and grow the layout around the Heart as your stockpiles allow.

  • II

    Hold the line

    Walls, gates and towers you position and upgrade, tested by waves that come for the Heart.

  • III

    Wake the Echoes

    Six essences the Heart kept. Each has a calling; you choose what each one gathers.

  • IV

    Take the Realm back

    Ride out from the village to raid the places the dark has claimed, then bring it home.
