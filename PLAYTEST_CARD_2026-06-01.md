## What's new this build
1. **First-time intro (FTUE).** If this is a fresh save, you should get a short guided intro
   when the village loads, and enemy waves should NOT start until the intro is done.
2. **Build Mode.** Tap the **Build** button on the village HUD. You can now:
   - Pick a structure from the bottom palette and tap the ground to place it (costs crystals).
   - **Tap a structure you already placed** → a little Move / Sell panel appears.
     - **Move** = pick it up and drop it somewhere new (free).
     - **Sell** = removes it and gives back half its crystal cost.
   - Tap **Exit** to leave Build Mode — your layout should still be there next time.
3. **The moat (water around the castle).** This is the big one to eyeball:
   - Does the water look like **calm water** now (not shiny "crystal/glass" shards)?
   - Do the **4 stone bridges** each lie **flat across the moat** at the gates —
     i.e. pointing OUT of town across the water, NOT turned sideways along the wall?
